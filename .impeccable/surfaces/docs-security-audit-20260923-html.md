# Security audit workbench

Mode: Operate / Read. Audience: Parkrr maintainers and security reviewers. Job: move from portfolio risk to one reproducible, remediable finding without exposing the report through the production application. Primary task: search, filter, inspect, deep-link, copy, export, and locally triage all 30 findings. The report remains a static development artifact with no external dependencies and no authoritative workflow state.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A risk register behaves like an evidence ledger: severity narrows attention, a compact rail selects the record, and one dossier carries the complete argument. It refuses the category-default wall of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Parkrr's ink-green surfaces, garage-plan linework, local Hanken/Bricolage/JetBrains type, restrained teal, and state-only severity colors. Controls remain familiar and flat.

STORY: The reviewer sees the total risk shape, finds a concern quickly, follows its causal chain, reproduces it, and leaves with a remediation queue or portable report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large thesis and audit provenance lead into a severity strip, then a three-part workbench: narrow filters, dense findings rail, wide dossier. The signature interaction is URL-addressable master-detail navigation with local triage; mobile turns the dossier into a focused full-screen layer.

FORM: Master-detail evidence ledger, candidate 3 of seven grounded structures and the dealt lead for surface seed `f49aae00` (API pool `c3b204a1eed6`). The brief's demand for an interactive version and Parkrr's established list/detail operational patterns corroborate the choice; the sneaker-wall challenger reinforced label-first scanning, while literal materials and motion were declined because they reduced security clarity.

FINISH: the final independent finish review records a `ship` verdict, and this surface brief remains the local design record for the standalone audit artifact. It introduces no root DESIGN.md, design sidecar, or raster-provenance requirement.
